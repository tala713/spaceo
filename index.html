⚖️ Weight & Thrust (W = m · g)

Every rocket has a strict payload mass limit. Exceeding capacity results in Thrust-to-Weight Ratio (TWR < 1), preventing liftoff.

⚡ Energy & Eclipse (E = P × t)

Solar panels generate zero power in shadow or lunar night. Batteries (Wh) must sustain heaters and comms throughout eclipse periods.

☀️ Inverse Square Law (1 / r²)

At Jupiter (780M km), solar intensity drops to 3.7% of Earth's. Deep space missions require Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generators (RTGs).

📡 Telemetry Rule

"Data that never reaches Earth never happened." Always include both a transceiver and an antenna to transmit scientific findings.
